# Figures

Future figures may include owned experiment captures, schematic diagrams, an original shader-architecture diagram, and controlled A/B comparisons. Only publish material that the contributor has the right to distribute. Do not add game screenshots, extracted textures, character models, third-party assets, or unlicensed video as repository figures.

Possible names for **future, real** figures are `pipeline-overview.svg`, `face-ablation-example.png`, and `validation-matrix.svg`. These are placeholders in the plan, not existing files or claimed results. A figure should identify its source, test conditions, and whether it shows an observation, schematic, or hypothesis.
